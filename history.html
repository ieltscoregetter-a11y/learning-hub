Discover the story, purpose and educational identity of Baku-Oxford School.

Our Story

Established in 1998, Baku-Oxford School is an independent, co-educational day school in Azerbaijan.

The school provides education for learners aged 3 to 18, combining national educational requirements with international curriculum standards.

English is the main language of instruction. The school brings together learners from different cultural and international backgrounds.
