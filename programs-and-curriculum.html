Primary School — Grades 1–5

Cambridge Primary

Cambridge Primary provides a curriculum framework that supports the development of knowledge, understanding and essential academic skills.

Key learning areas include:

  • English and literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Science and scientific enquiry
  • Social Studies and Humanities
  • Creative and physical development

Teachers can use practical tasks, classroom discussions, formative assessment and differentiated activities to support individual learning needs.

Languages & National Education

English is the principal language of instruction at Baku-Oxford School.

The school's published admissions guide also identifies Azerbaijani, Russian, French, German and Spanish among its language options.

Azerbaijan's National Context

Teachers should recognise Azerbaijan's history, language and cultural heritage while supporting international perspectives.

The published admissions guide identifies Grade 11 state diploma examinations for Azerbaijani students.

Teaching & Learning Practices

Inquiry-based Learning

Encourage students to ask questions, investigate evidence and construct understanding.

Curriculum Planning

Align learning objectives, classroom activities and assessment with relevant syllabus requirements.

Assessment for Learning

Use questioning, observation, exit tickets and feedback to identify learning needs.

Differentiated Instruction

Adjust scaffolding, resources and levels of support according to students' individual learning needs.

Higher-Order Thinking

Encourage students to analyse information, evaluate evidence and develop original solutions.

Inclusive Classrooms

Provide accessible instructions, appropriate learning support and meaningful participation opportunities for all learners.
