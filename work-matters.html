📋 Visa, Residence & Work Permits

Working in Azerbaijan

International employees should confirm their immigration and employment requirements before beginning work.

Where a work permit is required, the employer applies through the State Migration Service. Temporary residence requirements must also be considered.

Work Permit

Confirm with HR whether a work permit is required for your immigration status.

Temporary Residence

Review the applicable residence permit process and document requirements.

Documents to Prepare

The following is a preparation list. Confirm the documents required for your individual case with BOS HR.

  • Valid passport
  • University degree certificates
  • Academic transcripts
  • Teaching qualifications
  • Employment reference letters
  • Police clearance, if requested
  • Passport photographs
  • Medical documentation, where required
  • Marriage or birth certificates, if applicable

Document Legalisation & Translation

Requirements may vary according to the country where the document was issued.

  1. Confirm which documents require verification.
  2. Check applicable apostille or legalisation procedures.
  3. Arrange certified translations where requested.
  4. Submit documents through the approved HR process.
Ministry of Foreign Affairs →

Registration at Your Place of Stay

Foreign nationals generally need to register their place of stay when remaining in Azerbaijan for more than 15 days. Different rules may apply to certain nationalities.

🏠 Accommodation & Basic Needs

Finding a Home in Baku

Baku offers a range of furnished apartments in central and residential neighbourhoods.

Areas to explore include:

  • Yasamal and Elmlər Akademiyası
  • Səbail and the city centre
  • Nərimanov and Gənclik
  • Xətai and White City

Consider commuting time, public transport, utilities, nearby shops and the rental agreement when choosing accommodation.

BOS housing arrangements and allowances are subject to your contract.

✈️ First Flight & Arrival

Before Departure

  1. Confirm permitted entry and immigration arrangements.
  2. Share your flight details through the HR-approved channel.
  3. Keep necessary travel documents accessible.
  4. Confirm accommodation arrangements.
  5. Arrange airport transportation.
  6. Save your designated school contact information.

After Arrival in Baku

  1. Complete border entry procedures.
  2. Collect your luggage.
  3. Contact your designated school representative.
  4. Travel to your accommodation.
  5. Confirm registration and immigration next steps.
  6. Attend your scheduled school induction.
